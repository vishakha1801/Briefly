## Design Context

### Users
SaaS/tech sales reps — account executives at software companies closing $10k–$500k deals. They live in Salesforce and Slack, are used to high-quality product interfaces, and use SalesPilot *during* live calls. Context is focused: one window, high-stakes moment, minimal distraction budget.

### Brand Personality
Decisive, composed, credible. The tool should feel like a trusted co-pilot — present and sharp, not chatty or showy. Warm enough to feel human, precise enough to feel professional.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode. Sleek and premium with warmth underneath — references Linear/Vercel for precision, but with a human quality that feels alive during a conversation. Every element earns its place.

Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards (hero metrics, gradient accents, glassmorphism), anything that looks like it could be a CRM template.

### Design Principles
1. **Nothing decorative without purpose.** Every visual element must justify itself functionally.
2. **Cohesion through restraint.** Tint neutrals toward the brand hue — subconscious unity without loudness.
3. **Hierarchy through contrast, not color.** Size and weight do the work; color is reserved for state and emphasis.
4. **Precision in the details.** Spacing rhythm, hover states, and micro-transitions signal craft.
5. **The interface recedes; the call stays foregrounded.** Don't compete with the conversation.
